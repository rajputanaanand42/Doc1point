Questions

Good to know before you come.

Can't find your answer? Call us. A real person picks up.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead is the best way to make sure your doctor is available and to wait less.

When are the visiting specialists here?

Specialists from Patna, Delhi and Varanasi visit on set days. Call or WhatsApp us and we will tell you this week's schedule.

Can I get a lab test without seeing a doctor here?

Yes. Bring your prescription or tell us the test you need. Call ahead to check availability and the price.

How much discount do I get on medicines?

The discount depends on the medicine. Ask at the pharmacy counter or call us with your prescription.

Does the clinic run during power cuts?

Yes. We have power backup, so consultations, the lab and the waiting area keep running.

Can I get something to eat or drink while I wait?

Yes. Juice, cold drinks and biscuits are available at the clinic at a small charge.

I'm a doctor. How do I book a cabin?

Call us with the days and hours you would like, by the hour or for a full day. Once you're booked, our team manages your appointments, and tea and coffee are on us.
